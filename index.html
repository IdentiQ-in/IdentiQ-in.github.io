IdentiQ

Digital identity and professional networking platform for digital profiles, contact sharing, lead generation and engagement.

What is IdentiQ?

IdentiQ is a digital identity and professional networking platform that brings together digital profiles, digital business cards, NFC and QR sharing, contact exchange, portfolios, lead capture, CRM, analytics, consultations, storefronts, events and enterprise identity management.

The platform supports individuals, professionals, creators, consultants, sales teams, businesses, enterprises, Realtors, brokerages, hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafés and other professional and business use cases.
